# Size Guide

Choose your size by measuring your body, not a previous garment.

## How to measure
- **Chest:** around the fullest part of the chest.
- **Shoulder:** from one shoulder tip to the other across the back.
- **Length:** from the top of the shoulder down to the desired length.

Each product page has its own size guide, and you can request "tailored to your size" when the service is available.
