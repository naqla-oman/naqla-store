# Frequently Asked Questions

## How long does delivery take?
Usually two to three business days within Oman; faster delivery may be available in some areas and is shown at checkout.

## Can I pay on delivery?
Yes — the option appears at checkout whenever it is available for your order.

## How do I track my order?
From the [Track order](/track) page with your order number and phone number, or directly from your account.

## Can I exchange an item?
Yes, within {{returnDays}} days according to the [Exchange & Return Policy](/pages/returns).
